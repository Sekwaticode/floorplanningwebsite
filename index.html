About Masilela Planning

Masilela Planning is a Middelburg-based floor planning studio dedicated to turning your ideas into clear, buildable drawings. We work closely with homeowners, contractors and developers to design layouts that make sense for how you actually live and build.

From single-room additions to full residential and commercial developments, every plan we produce is drawn to scale, checked against local building regulations, and ready for council submission.

With years of experience serving Middelburg and the wider Mpumalanga region, our team combines technical accuracy with a genuine understanding of local building requirements — so your project moves from concept to construction without delays.
